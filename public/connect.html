Archive

Freeze the room: the chat stays readable, but Muses can’t check in and no new questions, keys, invites or members are allowed. You can unarchive any time; Muse keys keep working afterwards unless they expire.

Delete room

Permanently deletes this room and everything in it — members, Muse connections and keys, the chat, invite codes and history. This can’t be undone.
